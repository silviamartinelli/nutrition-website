# Eating with your cycle: what the evidence says

Social media is full of "cycle syncing" food rules. What does the research actually show?

## What is reasonably supported
- Energy needs and appetite can change slightly across the cycle.
- Iron matters if you have heavy periods.

## What is still uncertain
- Specific foods for each phase: evidence is limited.

## Takeaway
Listen to your body, keep meals balanced, and speak with a professional about persistent symptoms.
