# Blood sugar and mood

*Newsletter #9 · September 2026 (mock content – replace with your own)*

Have you ever felt shaky, irritable or anxious a couple of hours after a sugary breakfast? Rapid glucose swings trigger stress hormones such as adrenaline and cortisol, which can feel a lot like anxiety.

## Three simple strategies

1. **Pair carbohydrates** with protein, fat or fibre.
2. **Don't skip meals** if you notice mood dips when hungry.
3. **Move after meals**: even a 10-minute walk blunts the glucose peak.

> Note: this is general information, not medical advice.
