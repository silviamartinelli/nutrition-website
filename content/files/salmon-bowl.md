# Omega-3 salmon bowl

*Serves 2 · 20 minutes (mock recipe)*

## Ingredients
- 2 salmon fillets
- 150 g cooked quinoa
- 2 handfuls spinach
- 1 avocado
- Sauerkraut, sesame seeds, lemon

## Method
1. Pan-cook the salmon 4 minutes per side.
2. Assemble quinoa, spinach and avocado in bowls.
3. Top with salmon, sauerkraut and lemon.
