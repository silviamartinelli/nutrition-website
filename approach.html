Principles behind my method

1. Assess

Listen, measure, and identify patterns.

2. Prioritise

Small changes with the biggest effect first.

3. Personalise

Fit food to your preferences and culture.

4. Sustain

Build habits that last, not rules that break.

Frequently asked questions

Do you replace my doctor?

No. Coaching complements medical care. If needed I'll ask you to check in with your physician.


Do sessions take place online?

Yes, by video call, plus in-person where available. (Edit me!)


How many sessions will I need?

Most clients start with a 3-month package, then reassess.


Do you give meal plans?

I provide flexible guidance and example menus rather than rigid meal plans.
