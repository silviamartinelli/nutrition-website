Hi, I'm Dr. Silvia Martinelli, PhD. I'm a neurobiologist turned nutrition coach. (Write your personal story here: why you moved from research to coaching, what motivates you.)

My research explored how the brain responds to metabolic and hormonal signals. Today I use that knowledge to help people understand and improve the link between what they eat, how they feel and how their hormones behave.

I believe good nutrition advice should be accurate, individual and kind. That means reading the evidence carefully, being honest about what we don't yet know, and always treating you as the expert on your own life.
